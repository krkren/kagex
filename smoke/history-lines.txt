The extNagano plugin adds more transitions. Here are seven of them.
blurfade: a crossfade through a blur
flutter: the picture peels away like paper
scanline: lines slide in from alternate sides
3duniversal: a rule picture sends pieces flying, here shards
imagewipe: a wipe led by a picture, here a brush stroke
morphing: triangles warp the picture, here into a swirl (envinit.tjs)
honeyturn: hexagons flip over one by one
kagex's own glitch transition breaks the picture up on its way to the next.
glitch: a datamosh, a broken stream, damaged blocks, in turn
style=mosh: the old picture melts along the new one's motion
style=digital: lost blocks, a broken JPEG, sorted pixels
style=analog: a bad signal that rolls, slants and lags
And [glitch]: a moment of the same screen breaking up, here gentler and the same every
time.
Choice: Effekseer Effects
Glowing petals drift over the road: an Effekseer effect on a World layer, drawn by the
krkreffekseer plugin.
[Ai] Petals... that glow?
[Ai] And sparkles, all around me!
Each effect is a layer of its own: efk= names it, efkmag sizes it, and efkwidth and
efkheight set the size of its canvas, which is placed like any layer.
[Ai] Fireworks!
Their rockets go up about once a second, for as long as the layer plays.
[Ai] Hm? Something's glowing over there...
[Ai] Whoa!
A burst like that one plays once, and its layer clears when it's over.
With efkstop, an effect stops making particles and fades out.
[Ai] Now for an effect that goes wrong on purpose.
The picture breaks up into TV static: a static flip on a World layer at level=message,
drawn over the scene and this window at the layer's opacity.
[Ai] And the music... it's slowing down, like a tape running out!
pitch= changes a sound's speed and pitch together: here the music goes down to 35%
over two and a half seconds.
[Ai] Who just walked past?
pan= moves a sound between left and right: here footsteps cross from one side to the
other.
[Ai] Phew, everything's back to normal.
[Ai] Thanks for watching!
Choice: World Extension: Animated Character
Pip is drawn from layers, put together as it's shown: a body, two outfits, three arm poses
and six faces.
[Pip] Hi! I'm Pip.
[Pip] I blink by myself, and my mouth moves with my voice.
Its eyes blink every few seconds, and while it speaks, its mouth opens as wide as its
voice is loud.
[Pip] Hi again! Listen closely. When I speak with a real voice, my mouth makes the
shape of every vowel I say. Ah, ee, ooh, eh, oh. Isn't that so much better than mumbling?
For that line, kagex-lipsync worked out from the voice and its text when each vowel is
said, and wrote pip000009.lip beside the voice. Every other voice moves the mouth by its
loudness alone.
[Pip] Oh! A new face!
An x in front of a face, as in xsurprised, closes the eyes while the face changes.
[Pip] Oops, did I startle you?
[Pip] How do I look?
[Pip] Ha ha! I like it!
The laughing face has its eyes drawn shut, so they don't blink, while the mouth still
moves.
[Pip] Hey! I liked that hat!
Outfits change like faces: beret puts the hat on, and plain takes it off.
Each World level has its own set of layers, drawn at that size, so Pip stays sharp up
close.
The eyes and the mouth also take orders: eyecloseto closes the eyes and waits until
they're shut.
Then eyeopento opens them again.
eyeflip=3 blinks three times.
And lipflip=4 moves the mouth four times, without a voice.
[Pip] Bye for now!
That wave is an animation: waving, in data/main/charanim.csv, steps through the arm
poses on a timer until something else changes.
A CG from one layered file: the sky, the hills, Pip, its arms and its faces are layers, and
this picture shows some of them.
pipcg_laugh is the same file with other layers shown: arms up and a laughing face.
pipcg_evening has an evening sky, an orange light over everything, and the beret.
And pipcg_night the moon, the stars and a sleepy face. One file, four pictures, each in
the gallery by its own name.
This build doesn't play Live2D models. That takes the krkrlive2d plugin, built with
Live2D's Cubism SDK, and a model: "Live2D Characters" in doc/world.txt says how to
add them.
This is Vee, a VRM avatar: a 3D model in the format VRoid Studio exports, built for this
sample from simple shapes by a script, and drawn by the krkrthreepp plugin.
[Vee] Hi! I'm Vee. I'm a 3D model, so I can turn, wave and cheer. And when I talk, my
mouth makes the shape of every vowel, too!
Her mouth follows the lip track kagex-lipsync made from her voice: each vowel sets the
avatar's own expression for it, aa, ih, ou, ee or oh.
Her faces are weights for the avatar's expressions: laugh is all of happy, and smile less
of it.
VRM names the common ones, such as happy, sad, angry, relaxed and surprised, so a
face works for any avatar that has them.
And an avatar can have its own: blush is one of Vee's.
wave plays a VRM animation once, and motionwait waits for it to end; then her pose's
animation goes on.
A pose is a VRM animation that loops: think keeps her hand at her chin until another
pose comes.
One animation fades into the next, so nod starts from wherever the pose left her.
Her twin tails swing as she moves: VRM's spring bones.
