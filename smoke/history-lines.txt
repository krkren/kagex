flutter: the picture peels away like paper
blurfade: a crossfade through a blur
zoomfade: the picture zooms in as it fades

3duniversal: a rule picture sends pieces flying, here shards
honeyturn: hexagons flip over one by one

Choice: Effekseer Effects
Glowing petals drift over the road: an Effekseer effect on a World layer, drawn by the
krkreffekseer plugin.
[Ai] Petals... that glow?
[Ai] And sparkles, all around me!
Each effect is a layer of its own: efk= names it, efkmag sizes it, and efkwidth and efkheight set
the size of its canvas, which is placed like any layer.
[Ai] Fireworks!
Their rockets go up about once a second, for as long as the layer plays.
[Ai] Hm? Something's glowing over there...
[Ai] Whoa!
A burst like that one plays once, and its layer clears when it's over.
With efkstop, an effect stops making particles and fades out.
[Ai] Thanks for watching!
Choice: World Extension: Animated Character
Pip is drawn from layers, put together as it's shown: a body, two outfits, three arm poses and six
faces.
[Pip] Hi! I'm Pip.
[Pip] I blink by myself, and my mouth moves with my voice.
Its eyes blink every few seconds, and while it speaks, its mouth opens as wide as its voice is
loud.
[Pip] Hi again! Listen closely. When I speak with a real voice, my mouth makes the shape of
every vowel I say. Ah, ee, ooh, eh, oh. Isn't that so much better than mumbling?
For that line, kagex-lipsync worked out from the voice and its text when each vowel is said, and
wrote pip000009.lip beside the voice. Every other voice moves the mouth by its loudness alone.
[Pip] Oh! A new face!
An x in front of a face, as in xsurprised, closes the eyes while the face changes.
[Pip] Oops, did I startle you?
[Pip] How do I look?
[Pip] Ha ha! I like it!
The laughing face has its eyes drawn shut, so they don't blink, while the mouth still moves.
[Pip] Hey! I liked that hat!
Outfits change like faces: beret puts the hat on, and plain takes it off.
Each World level has its own set of layers, drawn at that size, so Pip stays sharp up close.
The eyes and the mouth also take orders: eyecloseto closes the eyes and waits until they're
shut.
Then eyeopento opens them again.
eyeflip=3 blinks three times.
And lipflip=4 moves the mouth four times, without a voice.
[Pip] Bye for now!
That wave is an animation: waving, in data/main/charanim.csv, steps through the arm poses on
a timer until something else changes.
This build doesn't play Live2D models. That takes the krkrlive2d plugin, built with Live2D's
Cubism SDK, and a model: "Live2D Characters" in doc/world.txt says how to add them.
