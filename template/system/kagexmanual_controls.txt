# The Controls
## Reading
- Go on: a click, a tap, Enter, Space, or the controller's A.
- The message history: {key:backlog}.
- Back to the line before: {key:back}.
- Skip what you've read: {key:skip}. Auto mode, which goes on by itself: {key:auto}.
- Hide the message window to see the picture: {key:hide}.
- Hear a voice again: {key:voiceReplay}.
## Menus
- Save: {key:save}. Load: {key:load}.
- Quick save: {key:quickSave}. Quick load: {key:quickLoad}.
- A screenshot: {key:screenshot}.
- This manual: {key:manual}.

In a window, the menu bar above the game has the rest; {key:menu} opens it from the keyboard or the controller.
## Phones and tablets
Tap to go on. A swipe or a long press does what the list of controls says, and the small strip of buttons by the message window has the menus.
## Every key and button
[See them all, and change them](action:controls): the list has every key, controller button and gesture, and Change Controls in it lets you give each action the keys you like.
