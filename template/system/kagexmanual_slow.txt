# The Game Is Slow, or Stutters
Try these, one at a time:
- [Reduce Motion](option:reduceMotion=true): effects that keep moving stand still, and gestures are smaller.
- [Skip transitions](option:noEffect=true): one picture changes to the next at once.
- [Smaller movies](option:movieQuality=small), if it's the movies that stutter.
- In a window, [a smaller window](option:windowSize=75) draws less.
- [Vertical sync on](option:vsync=true) evens the frames out; [off](option:vsync=false) may help a slow computer keep up.
- Close other programs, and on a laptop, plug it in: saving power slows the graphics down.
> In a web browser, the game fetches its pictures and sounds the first time it needs them, so the first play is slower than the next.
