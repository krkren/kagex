# The Settings
These links change a setting at once; a filled box shows the one in use. Config has them all.
## Text
- Text speed: [slow](option:textSpeed=3) [medium](option:textSpeed=6) [fast](option:textSpeed=9) [fastest](option:textSpeed=10)
- Skipping skips: [read text only](option:allSkip=false) [all text](option:allSkip=true)
- Read text marked: [yes](option:readMark=true) [no](option:readMark=false)
## Sound
- Music: [off](option:bgmVolume=0) [quiet](option:bgmVolume=40) [loud](option:bgmVolume=80) [full](option:bgmVolume=100)
- Voices: [off](option:voiceVolume=0) [quiet](option:voiceVolume=40) [loud](option:voiceVolume=80) [full](option:voiceVolume=100)
- A click while someone speaks: [stops the voice](option:voiceCut=stop) [fades it out](option:voiceCut=fade) [lets it finish](option:voiceCut=keep)
## The screen
- [Window](option:fullScreen=false) [Full screen](option:fullScreen=true)
- Transitions between pictures: [as they are](option:noEffect=false) [skipped](option:noEffect=true)
- [Reduce Motion](option:reduceMotion=true), or [everything moves](option:reduceMotion=false)
## More
[Open Config](action:config) for every setting, and [the controls](action:controls) to change the keys.
