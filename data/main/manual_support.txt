# Getting Help
The KAGEX sample is a showcase of the KAGEX framework. To report a problem or ask about it, open an issue at [github.com/krkren/kagex](url:https://github.com/krkren/kagex/issues), and say what you did, what you saw, and these:
- KAGEX {version}
- the engine: {engine}, on {platform}

[Copy these details](copy:KAGEX {version}, engine {engine}, {platform})

The game writes what it does into krkr.console.log, in {savefolder}; attach that file.
