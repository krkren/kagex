# The Demos
START on the title opens the demo menu. Each demo shows a part of KAGEX and its World extension, and its script (data/scenario) is worth reading beside it.

- KAGEX Extended Layer: layers that turn, zoom and move, movies in layers, vector pictures, 3D and Live2D on layers.
- World Extension: Character: Ai and Pip on the street, their voices, faces by the window, a flashback, a choice, and things that move with them.
- World Extension: Layer: the World's own layers, their actions and their stars.
- World Extension: Transition: every way one picture can change to the next.
- World Extension: Extended Action: actions that move, shake and spin.
- Effekseer Effects: sparkles, petals, fireworks and TV static.
- World Extension: Animated Character: Pip, who blinks and talks, a CG made from one layered file, and Hiyori and Vee where their plugins are.
- Text Effects: moving text, vertical text, rubies and pictures in the text.
- Credits: the credits, timed to the music.

Play six of them to the end, and Extras opens on the title: the CGs, the scenes to replay, the music, a search, the flowchart and Sprite Mode.
